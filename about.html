Who We Are

Built on Trust,
Driven by Purpose

Apro Global Real Estate was established in 2011 with the sole aim to bridge the gap between the demand and supply of quality housing, foster access to emerging real developments, below market price property investments and to offer real estate financial advisory services.

As a full service real estate agency and property management company, serving Lagos and Abuja, with a combined 23 years of experience we have demonstrated expertise in the management, sales and lease of; Flats/Apartments, Duplex, Townhouses, and Commercial properties — building a strong client base of over 1,500 serviced clients.
