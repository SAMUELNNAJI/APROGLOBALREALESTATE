Apro Global Real Estate was established in 2011 with the sole aim to bridge the gap between the demand and supply of quality housing, foster access to emerging real developments, below market price property investments and to offer real estate financial advisory services.

As a full service real estate agency and property management company, serving Lagos and Abuja, with a combined 23 years of experience in the real estate business we have demonstrated expertise in the management, sales and lease of; Flats/Apartments, Duplex, Townhouses, Commercial properties (shops, offices, warehouses) and more — building a strong client base of over 1,500 serviced clients.

We believe that prompt attention to our property seekers, property owners as well as our residents is crucial in delivering optimum service. We also believe in the success of our clients because we cannot spell success without "U". This has been our driving force that formed the basis of our vision: to "attain a global presence and be the first point of call in terms of real estate needs."
